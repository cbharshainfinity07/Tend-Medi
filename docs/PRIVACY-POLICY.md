# Tend — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 6 October 2026_

Tend is a medication reminder app. It is designed so that **your information never leaves your phone.**

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Tend has no account, no sign-in, no servers, no analytics and no advertising. We (the
makers of Tend) cannot see, receive or store any of your information.

## What stays on your phone

Everything you enter — medication names, doses, schedules, notes, dose history, refill counts and
any medicine photos you take — is stored only in Tend's private storage on your device. On Android,
release versions of Tend do not even have permission to use the internet.

## Permissions

- **Notifications** — to remind you when a dose is due. Reminders are scheduled on your device.
- **Camera / Photos** (optional) — only when you choose to photograph or pick a picture of a medicine. Photos are saved inside Tend, not shared.
- **Alarms & reminders / battery** (Android) — so reminders arrive at the exact minute.

## Backups and sharing

Tend can export a backup file or a CSV report **only when you ask it to**. You choose where that
file goes (for example Files, Google Drive or iCloud Drive). Once you share a file, the service you
send it to handles it under its own privacy policy.

## Deleting your data

Use **Settings → Erase all data** in Tend, or uninstall the app. Because nothing is stored anywhere
else, that removes everything.

## Children

Tend does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Medical disclaimer

Tend is a reminder tool and does not give medical advice. Always follow your doctor's or
pharmacist's instructions.
