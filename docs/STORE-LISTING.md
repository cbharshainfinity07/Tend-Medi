## Full description (both stores)

Every dose, kept.

Tend is a calm, private medication reminder for you and the people you care for. Reminders arrive
at the exact minute, taking a dose is one satisfying tap, and everything stays on your phone.

REMINDERS THAT FEEL GOOD
• Reminders right on time, with a gentle nudge if a dose isn't marked
• Mark taken, snooze or skip straight from the notification
• Hold to take, then watch your day fill up

KNOW EVERY MEDICINE ON SIGHT
• Photograph the tablet, strip or box
• See the photo on your reminders and on Today, full screen with one tap
• Perfect for anyone who finds medicine names hard to remember

EVERYTHING ELSE, HANDLED
• Any schedule: daily, every few hours, specific days, every few days or as needed
• Refill alerts before you run out
• History calendar, streaks and a report to share with your doctor
• Simple mode with bigger text and buttons
• Light and dark mode, screen reader and large text support

YOURS ALONE
• No account. No cloud. Works fully offline.
• Back up and restore through your phone's Files
• We never see your data — it never leaves your phone

Tend is a reminder tool and does not give medical advice. Always follow your doctor's or
pharmacist's instructions.
